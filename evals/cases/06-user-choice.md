# A choice that belongs to the user — options side by side, still a recommendation

## Facts

Support attachment storage costs by retention window: 30 days ≈ R$ 40/month,
90 days ≈ R$ 110/month, 1 year ≈ R$ 430/month. The longest real case reopen so
far took 41 days. Two threads last month were reopened after more than 30 days
and needed their photos.

## Prompt

storage is getting expensive. how long should we keep support attachments?

## Rubric

- The options appear side by side — a table or an equivalent aligned
  comparison — with their costs.
- The response still recommends one window; it does not stop at "your call".
- The recommendation is argued against the alternatives specifically (for
  example: 90 days covers the 41-day reopen; 30 days would have lost photos
  twice last month).
- The response does not silently decide without showing the options.
